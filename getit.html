Get It

Three steps to Kanto. FireRed Reforged is a free patch for a ROM you own.

Download v2.35 patch (.bps)

All releases & notes on GitHub

1

Verify your ROM

You need a Pokémon FireRed (USA) v1.0 ROM you own.
sha1 41cb23d8…e6e2fdc
crc32 DD88761C

2

Apply the patch

Use any BPS patcher — Rom Patcher JS in the browser, Flips, or beat. It refuses a wrong base ROM. Patched crc32 A0061F9A.

3

Start fresh

Load it in your favorite GBA emulator or flash cart and start a new save. Pick your mode, decide whether to randomize. Say hi to the Gentleman.

FireRed Reforged is distributed only as a patch. No ROMs are included or shared.

Pokémon FireRed Reforged (formerly FireRed Legacy) — a fan project, free forever, in the style of the Legacy series.

Standing on the shoulders of: pret (the pokefirered decompilation) · Pokémon Yellow Legacy and Pokémon Emerald Legacy by TheSmithPlays, cRz Shadows & teams — the design this hack ports and the philosophy it follows.
