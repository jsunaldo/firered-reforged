Mechanics & Items

Engine and item-placement changes, hand-verified against the Emerald Legacy and Yellow Legacy sources.

Battle mechanics

  • Ghost / Dark category swap (Emerald Legacy parity): Ghost moves are special, Dark moves are physical — Gengar's Shadow Ball and Tyranitar's Crunch finally use the right stats.
  • Hail now grants Ice-types a 1.5× Defense boost; sandstorm grants Rock-types (and Sand Veil Pokémon) 1.5× Sp. Def.
  • Magma Armor massively blunts Water-type damage (Sp. Atk ÷8), with an in-battle message when it triggers.
  • Trade evolutions removed: Kadabra/Haunter evolve at Lv 42, Machoke/Graveler at Lv 38; Metal Coat, King's Rock, Dragon Scale, Up-Grade, Deep Sea items are used from the bag like stones. Feebas evolves at Lv 35 (beauty doesn't exist in FRLG).
  • Modes: Normal / Hard chosen at the start (Yellow Legacy parity). Hard = badge level caps 12/21/24/35/43/50/53/55/65, no bag items in trainer battles, SET style; caps lift after the Champion.
  • Mew obeys — the Mew-quest reward is a legitimate, obedient Mew.

Key items & shops

  • Old Rod: Fishing Guru inside Viridian City's Mart (Yellow Legacy move — fish before badge one).
  • Good Rod: the Vermilion City fishing house (was Old Rod's spot).
  • Fuchsia fishing house: gifts the opposite Mt. Moon fossil Pokémon at Lv 10 — both fossils obtainable in one save.
  • Super Rod: Route 12 house (unchanged).
  • Exp. Share: the Route 15 gate aide gives it with no catch requirement.
  • TM35 Flamethrower: item ball in the Rocket Hideout B4F (was TM49).
  • Silph Co. Lapras: now Lv 35 (was 25).
  • Brick Piece: from Master Koichi at the Saffron Dojo (Tyrogue's evolution item).
  • Indigo Plateau TM shop: Ice Beam, Thunderbolt, Flamethrower, Giga Drain, SolarBeam, Brick Break, Rock Tomb (Yellow Legacy's league shop).
  • Two Island market (post-Champion): evolution stones/items, Sea & Lax Incense.
  • All mart inventories match Yellow Legacy's, translated to Gen 3 items.
