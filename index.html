Pokémon FireRed Reforged

Same Kanto, Same StoryA slight blend of Yellow and Red/Blue. Professor Oak gives you one of the three starter Pokémon and your rival gets an Eevee. The main story through the Elite Four matches FireRed's, but the postgame adds many additional twists and turns!
Upgraded PostgameJohto & Hoenn leaders on the Johto Isles, gym rematches, Elite Four rematches, additional new trainers, and more. Game ModesHard mode with enforced level caps and perma-death. A randomizer built in. An optional Pallet Town NPC who gives key supplies.
Trainer Teams300+ trainer teams rebuilt, largely based on the ideology of Yellow Legacy by the SmithPlaysPokemon team.
Wild EncountersAll wild encounters updated, again heavily inspired by Yellow Legacy's decisions. All 386 Pokémon are obtainable.
Gym OrderErika, Koga, Sabrina, and Blaine can be battled in any order. Their teams adjust based on the order you battle them.
Level CurveLevels climb smoothly with updated trainer teams.
Meet the AceEvery leader and Elite Four member stands beside their signature Pokémon.
