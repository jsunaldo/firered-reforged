Same Kanto, Same Story

A slight blend of Yellow and Red/Blue. Professor Oak gives you one of the three starter Pokémon and your rival gets an Eevee. The main story through the Elite Four matches FireRed's, but the postgame adds many additional twists and turns!

Upgraded Postgame

Johto & Hoenn leaders on the Johto Isles, gym rematches, Elite Four rematches, additional new trainers, and more.

Game Modes

Hard mode with enforced level caps and perma-death. A randomizer built in. An optional Pallet Town NPC who gives key supplies.

Trainer Teams

300+ trainer teams rebuilt, largely based on the ideology of Yellow Legacy by the SmithPlaysPokemon team.

Wild Encounters

All wild encounters updated, again heavily inspired by Yellow Legacy's decisions. All 386 Pokémon are obtainable.

Gym Order

Brock, Misty and Lt. Surge come first, in that order, and Giovanni last. A guard on Route 9 holds the road to Rock Tunnel until you have three badges. After that, Erika is open right away, Koga waits for the Poké Flute from the Pokémon Tower, and Sabrina's gym stays blocked until you clear Silph Co. Blaine's island needs Surf, which takes 5 badges, so he's always 6th or 7th. Erika, Koga, Sabrina and Blaine bring teams matched to how many badges you hold.
