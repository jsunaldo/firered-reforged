The idea

Same game. Better game.

FireRed Reforged follows TheSmithPlays' Legacy trilogy: keep the Gen 3 soul and fix everything else. Kanto's trainers, wild Pokémon and key items follow Yellow Legacy; every stat, moveset and evolution follows Emerald Legacy.

  • 2.12 — The Pokémon beside the Champion is his real ace for that fight, randomized teams included. It used to always be an Eevee.

All releases & notes

1

Verify your ROM

You need a Pokémon FireRed (USA) v1.0 ROM you own.
sha1 41cb23d8…e6e2fdc
crc32 DD88761C

2

Apply the patch

Use any BPS patcher — Rom Patcher JS in the browser, Flips, or beat. The patch refuses a wrong base ROM. Patched crc32 E1BB1C11.

3

Start fresh

Load it in your favorite GBA emulator or flash cart and start a new save. Pick your mode, decide whether to randomize. Say hi to the Gentleman.

FireRed Reforged is distributed only as a patch. No ROMs are included or shared.
